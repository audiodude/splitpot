## Hold'em on a 36-card deck, 2-5 removed, flush over boat

**Rarity: low.** Ante-only, [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) or [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), no verified examples (evaluator can't rank it).

## Cheat sheet

- 6 through ace. Best five of seven, same as Hold'em.
- Ranking (WSOP): flush > full house, straight > trips.
- Ace plays low in A-6-7-8-9.
- Everyone [antes](/glossary#ante), [button](/glossary#button) posts double. No [blinds](/glossary#blinds).
- WSOP pre-flop: fold or exactly 3x, no raise, limp or straddle. Online/Triton: normal raising, limping.
- WSOP seat caps: 9 for Hold'em, 6 for short-deck PLO.

## Watch out for

- **Trips vs straight:** WSOP, PokerNews, PokerCoaching say straight wins. PokerListings says trips, and some rooms agree. Ask.
- Boat-over-flush muscle memory.
- Pocket pairs and top pair are weaker, draws stronger (outs rule of thumb 3 and 6).
- "Manila" is a different 32-card game, though Wikipedia lists it as an alias.
