## [Omaha Hi-Lo](/games/omaha-hi-lo), but the low is [2-7](/glossary#27-low) with a 9 [qualifier](/glossary#qualifier)

[Two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), four hole cards, high half and low half. WSOP: "9 qualifier and is played as a 2-7 hand. When playing low, Aces are high, straights and flushes count against you." Eight players max. WSOP is the only source we found.

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards, exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each half; different pairs allowed.
- Low: 2-7 ranking, ace high, straights, flushes and pairs hurt. Nuts: 7-5-4-3-2 offsuit.
- No qualifying low: high [scoops](/glossary#scoop). Ties get [quartered](/glossary#quartered). [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip) to the high half.
- No betting structure in the WSOP text; limit or pot-limit is our inference.

## Watch out for

- "9 qualifier" isn't defined for 2-7. Our reading: nine-high or better, no pair, straight or flush (worst qualifier 9-8-7-6-4). Unverified, so ask.
- A-2 is not premium; wheel-type hands are ace-high junk for the low.
- Don't mix it up with Omaha Hi-Lo variants that cap an *ace-to-five* low at 9 or 7.
