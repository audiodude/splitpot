## Six cards, split 2 + 4, one board, two high halves

**Rarity: low.** Home game, BARGE and Card Player Lifestyle only.

## Cheat sheet

- Six hole cards, blinds and button, single board.
- Split into a Hold'em hand (2) and an Omaha hand (4) after pre-flop betting (BARGE). Locked.
- Hold'em half plays any of two plus board. Omaha half uses the [Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule).
- Both halves high. Win both to [scoop](/glossary#scoop).
- BARGE: [limit](/glossary#limit), small bet through flop, big bet turn and river.

## Watch out for

- Split timing: BARGE after pre-flop, Card Player Lifestyle before the flop, Haney during the first betting round.
- Hands share no cards.
- Pair-in-Omaha with a weak Hold'em two is a trap.
- Slow game.
