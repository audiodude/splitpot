## 2-7 triple draw structure, [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) rankings

Five cards, three [draws](/glossary#draw), small-small-big-big [limit](/glossary#limit), six max. Lowest hand by [ace-to-five](/glossary#a5-low) takes it all. No qualifier.

## Cheat sheet

- Aces low, straights and flushes ignored, pairs bad. Nuts: the [wheel](/glossary#wheel), 5-4-3-2-A any suits. Then 6-4-3-2-A, 6-5-3-2-A, 6-5-4-2-A, 6-5-4-3-A, 6-5-4-3-2, 7-4-3-2-A.
- Compare from the top card down. Paired hands: lower pair wins (A-A beats 2-2).
- Same procedure as 2-7 triple draw: counts locked after discards, burn before every draw, post-draw action left of the button, big bet after the second draw.
- Tournament odd chip: first seat left of the button.

## Watch out for

- 2-7 reflexes. Here a 6-5-4-3-2 "straight" is a six-low and a suited wheel is the nuts.
- [Check-raise](/glossary#check-raise): WSOP live-action allows it; Robert's bans it in limit A-5. [Sevens rule](/glossary#sevens-rule): Robert's assumes it, the WSOP doesn't list it, and no source documents it for triple draw. Page doesn't use it.
- [Joker](/glossary#lowball-joker): Robert's assumes one unless posted; WSOP assumes none. The mixed-game version is 52 cards.
- Exposed cards on the deal: WSOP replaces all; Robert's keeps a seven or lower.
- Not Razz: five cards, you must use all of them.
