## [California lowball](/games/california-lowball), big-bet edition

[A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low), one [draw](/glossary#draw), [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) or [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit). Lowest hand takes it all.

## Cheat sheet

- Ace low, straights and flushes ignored, pairs bad. Nuts: the [wheel](/glossary#wheel).
- Open or fold pre-draw. Big blind may be callable, or the open must be 2x BB.
- No [sevens rule](/glossary#sevens-rule). Any hand may check after the draw. [Check-raise](/glossary#check-raise) allowed (WSOP 282, Robert's NL/PL 5).
- Draw 0-5. WSOP: push-and-pull, a card that lands exposed on a pitch stays with you. Exactly five cards after the draw or the hand is fouled.
- [Joker](/glossary#lowball-joker) only if posted at the WSOP; Robert's assumes it. It's the lowest card not in your hand.
- Ties split. Six max.

## Watch out for

- 2-7 instincts: here the wheel is the nuts and flushes are free.
- Exposed card before the draw: Robert's keeps an A, 2, 3, 4 or 5; the WSOP replaces every one.
- Draw size: Robert's four then one; WSOP five in a row.
- Say "pair" when you spread one (rules 279-280).
