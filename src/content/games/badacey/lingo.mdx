## A-5 triple draw with a [badugi](/glossary#badugi) half, [aces low](/glossary#a5-low) in both

Five cards, three [draws](/glossary#draw), small-small-big-big [limit](/glossary#limit), six max. Half the pot to the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low), half to the best badugi. [No qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier).

## Cheat sheet

- Low half: five cards, ace low, straights and flushes ignored. Nuts: the [wheel](/glossary#wheel).
- Badugi half: best four-or-fewer cards, different ranks and suits. More cards beats fewer, then lowest high card. Nuts: A-2-3-4 [rainbow](/glossary#rainbow).
- The one hand unbeatable in both halves (WSOP rule 316): A, 2, 3, 4 of four different suits plus a 5 of any suit. Best case is a [scoop](/glossary#scoop).
- Halves are independent, so you can be [quartered](/glossary#quartered). Odd chip is by position (inference: the Badacey section is silent).
- Draw 0-5, up to five in a row. Joining: wait for the BB or post a full BB.

## Watch out for

- [One-way hands](/glossary#one-way-hand) like 2♥4♥6♥7♥A♣: nut-ish low, two-card badugi, easy to [freeroll](/glossary#freeroll).
- Not Badeucy: ace low in both halves, and no 2-7 straight or flush penalty.
- No eight-or-better rule here (BARGE says so explicitly).
- Spelling: Badacey, Badacy, Badaci (WSOP). Ante vs blinds: blinds in rooms, ante only per Wizard of Odds. Draw cap: five (WSOP).
- Pat 8-badugis lose to the stronger badugis that five-card hands produce.
