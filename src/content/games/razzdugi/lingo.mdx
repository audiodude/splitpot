## [Razz](/games/razz) plus a [badugi](/glossary#badugi) half, both made from your seven cards

Stud structure as in Razz: [ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), [limit](/glossary#limit), seven cards. Half the pot to the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low), half to the best ace-low badugi. [No qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier) on either half.

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Pick five cards for the razz hand and up to four for the badugi, from the same seven.
- Badugi: different ranks *and* different suits, ace low, more cards beats fewer. If nobody has four, three cards wins, then two, then one. Nuts: A-2-3-4 [rainbow](/glossary#rainbow).
- Bring-in: **highest** [door card](/glossary#door-card), ace low. K♠ is automatic (BARGE). May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: **lowest** razz board acts first. [Open pair](/glossary#open-pair) doesn't change the bet size.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth to seventh. Check-raise allowed. Up to eight players.
- [Scoop](/glossary#scoop) needs both halves, e.g. A-2-3-4 four-suited plus a 5.
- Ties split the half. Odd chip goes to the razz hand, because badugi has no five-card hand.
- Variants: "super" (four down, discard two) is described by Haney, but no rule source gives a procedure. Action Razzdugi: razz half needs a J/Q/K among your cards.

## Watch out for

- A repeated suit kills your badugi. It doesn't hurt your razz hand.
- A pair can hurt razz but not badugi. A suited wheel is the nuts in razz and a three-card badugi.
- Both halves are always awarded. A junk three-card badugi can win.
- Don't confuse it with [Razzdeucey](/games/razzdeucey): that one is 2-7 low plus ace-*high* badugi.
- Haney calls this game "Razzdacey" and says it used to be "Super Razzdugi".
