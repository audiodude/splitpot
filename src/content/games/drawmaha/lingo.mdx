## Omaha High plus a five-card high [draw](/glossary#draw) half

Five hole cards, flop, one draw (1 to 3 cards at the WSOP), turn, river. Pot splits: half to the best Omaha high ([exactly two from hand and three from the board](/glossary#omaha-rule)), half to the best five-card high hand from your own cards only. Usually [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit) or [limit](/glossary#limit).

## Cheat sheet

- Draw half: all five of your final cards, board ignored, ordinary high rankings.
- Omaha half: exactly two hole cards and three board cards.
- Only your final five count, so discards are gone for good.
- Draw procedure varies by room: draw cap (1-3 at the WSOP, 0-5 elsewhere) and whether the turn comes before or after the draw cards.
- Sviten Special: a one-card draw is dealt face up, and you can reject it.

## Watch out for

- Breaking a great Omaha holding to draw.
- One-way hands: big Omaha cards with no draw hand, or a pat draw hand that can't make an Omaha hand.
- A pair of aces wins the draw half but may lose the Omaha half to a better straight made with different hole cards.
