## Hold'em with three hole cards and one discard

Deal three, discard one, then it's regular [Hold'em](/games/no-limit-holdem): best five of any two kept cards plus the board.

## Cheat sheet

- Three hole cards. Discard one dead card.
- WSOP timing: discard **after** the pre-flop betting round, **before** the flop.
- Everything else is Hold'em. Any structure.
- High only. Zero, one, or two hole cards play. Ties split, odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) by position from the button.

## Watch out for

- Timing disagrees: Pagat, Wikipedia, Wizard of Odds, and BeatTheFish discard before any betting. Ask which version is being called.
- Top pair and two pair are overvalued with three-card starts.
- No "exactly two" rule here.
- Not OFC Pineapple.
