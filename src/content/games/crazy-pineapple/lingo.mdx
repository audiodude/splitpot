## Pineapple, but the discard comes after the flop betting

Three hole cards through pre-flop and flop betting, then discard one before the turn. After that, plain Hold'em.

## Cheat sheet

- Discard after the flop betting round, before the turn (WSOP, Pagat, Wikibooks, BeatTheFish). Wizard of Odds has it right after the flop, before flop betting.
- High only per the WSOP. Hi-lo 8-or-better is common elsewhere and is a different game (Crazy Pineapple Hi-Lo).
- Zero, one, or two hole cards play. [Playing the board](/glossary#playing-the-board) is legal. Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) by position from the button.
- Limit, pot-limit, or no-limit.
- Mix slot: C.H.O.R.S.E. adds "Chowaha or Crazy Pineapple" to H.O.R.S.E.

## Watch out for

- Discarding before the flop (that's Pineapple) or after the turn (too late).
- Keeping the pre-flop-pretty card instead of the one that fits the flop.
- Top pair and two pair are overvalued with three-card starts. Chase the [nuts](/glossary#nuts).
- Lazy Pineapple / Tahoe and Super Hold'em are other games.
