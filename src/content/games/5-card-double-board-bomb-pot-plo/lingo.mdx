## Double board [bomb pot](/glossary#bomb-pot) PLO5

[Ante](/glossary#ante)-only, no pre-flop action. WSOP-defined. **Rarity: low to moderate.**

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards, [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), 8-max.
- No blinds, no pre-flop betting, no [burn](/glossary#burn-card) before the flop. Action starts left of the button.
- Each board: exactly 2 hole + 3 board ([Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule)). Half the pot per board.
- Eight players uses the entire deck; last card is the river.
- Premature river with two cards left: reshuffle last card, premature card, burns and muck.
- Default bomb-pot ante if undecided: $20 (1-2-5), $25 ($5-$5 and up).

## Watch out for

- Odd chip not specified by WSOP; TDA default is first seat left of the button.
- Button passes to the next poster if the worst position doesn't play.
- Only WSOP documents the five-card rules.
