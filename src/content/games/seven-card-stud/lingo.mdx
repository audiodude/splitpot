## Seven cards, no board, five [streets](/glossary#street), best five-card high wins

Seven-card stud: [ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), face-up cards, [limit](/glossary#limit). Straight high-hand ranking, no qualifier, whole pot to one hand (or a chop).

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, one up ([door card](/glossary#door-card)), then up, up, up, down. Best five of seven.
- Third street: lowest door card brings it in. Ace is **high**, so 2♣ is the lowest. Ties by suit (clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades). The bring-in player may [complete](/glossary#complete) to a full small bet.
- Fourth street and later: best *showing* poker hand acts first. Ties: closest to the dealer's left (WSOP; TDA says high card by suit).
- Limit: [small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth through seventh. WSOP cap: bet plus four raises, completion not counted. Robert's, Pagat and PokerNews: three raises.
- [Open pair](/glossary#open-pair) on fourth street: WSOP and Robert's let anyone bet or raise either size. TDA and many rooms say no. Not a thing in Stud 8 or [Razz](/glossary#razz).
- Eight players and the stub runs short: one face-up [community card](/glossary#community-card) for seventh street.
- Folded seats keep getting dealt cards until a bet kills the hand. Turning up your up cards facing a bet is a fold.
- Odd chip: highest card by suit among the five-card hand (WSOP).

## Watch out for

- No position. First action is set by the up cards, and it moves every street.
- Ace high for the bring-in, but it still plays low in a wheel.
- Straights and flushes can't show on the board with four up cards. A visible hand is only ever pairs, trips, or high cards.
- Track [dead cards](/glossary#dead-card) in folded hands, too.
- Don't reorder your up cards. It can get a hand ruled dead.
