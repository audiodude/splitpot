## 5-card PLO with the first flop card face up before pre-flop betting

[Pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), [exactly two plus three](/glossary#omaha-rule), high only.

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards, then the first flop card is exposed (no burn before it at the WSOP), then pre-flop betting.
- After pre-flop: burn, two more flop cards. Then turn, river, one burn each.
- The [door card](/glossary#courchevel-door-card) is a normal flop card. It stays on the board.
- 8 players max at the WSOP. [INFERENCE for why: 9 x 5 + door + 3 burns + 4 board = 53.]
- Pre-flop action starts left of the big blind.
- Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) by position clockwise from the button.

## Watch out for

- Overvaluing a pre-flop set made with the door card.
- Burn procedure varies: WSOP has none before the door card, Mixed Game Masters says there is one.
- Playing PLO5 pre-flop ranges when a third of the flop is known.
