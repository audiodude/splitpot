## [Big O](/games/big-o) with a floating card count

Five-plus hole cards, same number for everyone, count tied to players dealt in. Everything else is [Omaha Hi-Lo](/games/omaha-hi-lo): [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low, high [scoops](/glossary#scoop) without a qualifier. WSOP: 8 max.

## Cheat sheet

- At least 5 hole cards (5 = 10 combos, 6 = 15, 7 = 21, 8 = 28). Same count for every player.
- Exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each of high and low; different pairs allowed.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, ace low, [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low.
- Structure isn't specified by the WSOP; pot-limit is the Big O norm.
- [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip): high half under the WSOP's general rule.

## Watch out for

- Card count rooms disagree: WSOP says 5 minimum; a 2012 report says button picks 4-6. Ask.
- Near-nut lows are everywhere, so [counterfeits](/glossary#counterfeit) and quarters are routine.
- Only appears in the WSOP live-action sheet and Dealer's Choice. Don't assume the house knows it.
