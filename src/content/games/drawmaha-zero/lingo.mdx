## Drawmaha with a low [pip count](/glossary#pip-count) draw half

[Drawmaha](/games/drawmaha) skeleton: five hole cards, flop, one [draw](/glossary#draw), turn, river. Half to Omaha high ([2-and-3](/glossary#omaha-rule)), half to the lowest point total on your five final cards, board ignored. [Limit](/glossary#limit) or [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit). It's the mirror of [Drawmaha 49](/games/drawmaha-49), and it isn't in the WSOP rule text.

## Cheat sheet

- Points: A = 1, 2 to T = face value, J/Q/K = 0. Lowest total wins. Best: five faces (0). Worst: T-T-T-T-9 (49). Suits and poker hands irrelevant. Equal totals split the half.
- Tens are 10, not 0. Both Haney articles call it the common mistake.
- Omaha half: exactly two hole cards plus three board, high. A player can [scoop](/glossary#scoop).
- Draw: BARGE 0 to 3; home games and some tables allow more or cap at 2 (Draw2maha). Table-dependent, so ask.
- [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip): the WSOP convention is first player clockwise from the button, but that rule doesn't name Zero, so check with the dealer.
- 16 premium cards (A and faces) are also the good Omaha cards, so scoops come up more than in 49. Haney rates it 8/10, the best of the point games.

## Watch out for

- Counting tens as zero.
- Mixing it up with 49 in a rotation. Forum posters report doing exactly that, so don't deal them back-to-back.
- Ace is 1 point and the best Omaha card: it's doubly valuable, so don't throw it for a face card.
- Keeping a pair in the points half. Poker rank doesn't count there. A [one-way hand](/glossary#one-way-hand) is far less common here than in 49.
- Zero cousins: [Drawmaha 21](/games/drawmaha-21) (home game and BARGE), Red/Black and Dugi 2.0 (home-only per Haney).

## Examples

Final hands: Dee K♣ Q♦ J♠ A♥ 5♠ = 6, Eli A♦ K♠ 2♣ 3♦ Q♠ = 6 (after a one-card draw replacing a 9♣, Haney's example hand), Fay T♥ T♠ Q♥ J♥ 4♦ = 24. Dee and Eli tie for the Zero half and split it. Fay's pair of tens is irrelevant on this half.
