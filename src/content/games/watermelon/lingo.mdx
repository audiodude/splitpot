## Five-card Omaha, three boards, majority of boards wins the whole pot

**Rarity: very low, single-source.**

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards, three boards, [Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule) per board (inferred).
- Board win = 1 point, board tie = half a point each.
- Most points scoops the pot. Points tie means the tied players share it.
- Structure, burn procedure and odd chip are not stated. [INFERENCE] [Pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit).

## Watch out for

- Not paid per board.
- 1/1/1 means a three-way chop.
- Don't confuse with an older Pineapple-related "Watermelon" a forum title hints at (unverified).
