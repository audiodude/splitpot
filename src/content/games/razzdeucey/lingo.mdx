## [Razzdugi](/games/razzdugi) in [2-7](/glossary#27-low) clothing: stud Badeucy

Seven-card stud structure ([ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), [limit](/glossary#limit)). Half the pot to the best 2-7 low, half to the best aces-high [badugi](/glossary#badugi). [No qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier) on either half. Despite the name, no A-5 Razz.

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Pick five for the 2-7 hand and up to four for the badugi, from the same seven.
- 2-7: aces high, straights and flushes count against you, pairs bad. Nuts: 7-5-4-3-2, not all one suit.
- Badugi: different ranks and suits, **aces high**. Nuts: 5-4-3-2 [rainbow](/glossary#rainbow). More cards beats fewer.
- Double nut: 2-3-4-5-7 with four suits. That's the [scoop](/glossary#scoop).
- Bring-in: **highest** [door card](/glossary#door-card), so A♠ is automatic. May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: **lowest** 2-7 board acts first. Four up cards can't show a straight or a flush.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth to seventh. Check-raise allowed, up to eight players.
- Odd chip goes to the five-card (2-7) hand (WSOP rule 153 for Razzdugi/Razzduci).
- "Super" (four down, discard two) is described by Haney as the usual way to play it, but no rule source gives a procedure. "(Three Cards Down)" at Resorts World is probably discard-one. [INFERENCE]

## Watch out for

- The WSOP sheet contradicts itself: its paragraph says badugi + 2-7, rule 333 says 2-7 + razz, rule 332 describes an A-2-3-4-5 best hand. This page follows the paragraph, Haney and Card Player Lifestyle.
- A-2-3-4 is the *worst* four-card badugi here, not the best.
- The ace door card is the worst card and the bring-in.
- 3-4-5-6-7 is a straight, so it's a bad low.
- Don't confuse with [Razzdugi](/games/razzdugi) (A-5 low + ace-low badugi). Haney describes people nearly playing a whole round of the wrong one.
