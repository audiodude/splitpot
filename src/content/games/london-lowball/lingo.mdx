## Razz with [A-6 ranking](/glossary#a6-low) (straights and flushes count against you), played [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit)

Razz deal, razz [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in) (highest [door card](/glossary#door-card), ace low, K♠ highest), lowest board acts first, five rounds, lowest five of seven wins, no qualifier.

## Cheat sheet

- Nuts: 6-4-3-2-A offsuit. 5-4-3-2-A is a straight, so it's bad.
- Pot-limit: min bet = bring-in, min raise = last bet or raise, max = pot (BARGE).
- No joker. Ties split.
- **Very rare.** Seen in London clubs and at BARGE (first in 2018). In no US room rulebook I found.

## Watch out for

- Playing it fixed-limit.
- Treating A-2-3-4-5 as the nuts.
- Confusing it with draw ace-to-six lowball.
