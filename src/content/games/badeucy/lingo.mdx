## 2-7 triple draw with a [badugi](/glossary#badugi) half

Same structure as 2-7 triple draw: five cards, three [draws](/glossary#draw), small-small-big-big, [limit](/glossary#limit), six max. Half the pot goes to the best [2-7 low](/glossary#27-low), half to the best badugi. **Aces are high in both halves.**

## Cheat sheet

- 2-7 half: five cards, aces high, straights and flushes count against you. Nuts: 7-5-4-3-2 offsuit.
- Badugi half: best four-or-fewer cards, all different suits and ranks. More cards beats fewer, then lowest high card wins. Ace is the top card, so the nuts is 5-4-3-2 rainbow. The double-nut hand is 2-3-4-5-7 with four suits.
- One player can [scoop](/glossary#scoop). Tied halves get [quartered](/glossary#quartered).

## Watch out for

- Playing the ace as low in the badugi half (that's Badacey, a different game).
- Standing [pat](/glossary#snow) with a nut 2-7 and no badugi: you get [freerolled](/glossary#freeroll) by a player drawing to a badugi.
- 6-5-4-3-2 is a straight, so it's a bad low.
