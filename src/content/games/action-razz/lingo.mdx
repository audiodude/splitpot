## [Razz](/glossary#a5-low) with a face-card qualifier

Razz deal and [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in) (highest [door card](/glossary#door-card)), small/small/big/big/big. To qualify you need a J, Q or K ([paint](/glossary#paint)) among your seven cards. Qualifier beats non-qualifier; among qualifiers, best A-5 low wins.

## Cheat sheet

- The paint doesn't have to be in your five-card low.
- Nobody qualifies, or everyone does: plain razz decides (BARGE, Raymer).
- Wheel without paint loses to queens full of kings.
- Origin: BARGE, summer 2020, Kevin Un (Raymer, BARGE rulebook). **Rare**, and not in WSOP, Robert's or TDA.

## Watch out for

- Card Player Lifestyle says that if nobody qualifies the pot is split. BARGE and Raymer say plain razz.
- Haney names the 10♠ as the bring-in, BARGE and Raymer say the K♠.
- Haney suggests only two down cards for the "super" version.
