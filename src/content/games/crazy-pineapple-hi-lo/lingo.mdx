## [Crazy Pineapple](/games/crazy-pineapple) with an [8-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low half

Three hole cards, discard one after the flop betting, then Hold'em. Showdown is any five of seven, no [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), so you can [play the board](/glossary#playing-the-board). Pot splits between high and an A-5 low.

## Cheat sheet

- Three down, bet, flop, bet, **discard to two**, turn, bet, river, bet. Usually [limit](/glossary#limit).
- High and low each use any five of seven; different five-card hands allowed.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, aces low, straights and flushes don't count against you. The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low. No qualifying low: high [scoops](/glossary#scoop).
- Three board cards above 8 = no low possible.
- Ties get [quartered](/glossary#quartered). [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip): high half in a hi/lo split.
- Deuces Wild rules: up to 11 players, a failure to discard fouls your hand.

## Watch out for

- Discard timing differs by source: Pagat and Deuces Wild say after flop betting, Wizard of Odds says before. This page uses after.
- The WSOP lists Crazy Pineapple as high-only, so the hi-lo form is a Pagat and card-room game.
- A board with five distinct cards 8 or lower is a low all by itself, so it gets chopped unless someone holds a better one.
