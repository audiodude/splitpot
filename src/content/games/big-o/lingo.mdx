## [Omaha Hi-Lo](/games/omaha-hi-lo) with five hole cards

Same [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), same [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low), same [scoop](/glossary#scoop) when nobody qualifies. You just hold ten two-card combos instead of six. WSOP rules, eight players max.

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards, flop/turn/river. Usually [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), sometimes [limit](/glossary#limit).
- Exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each of high and low, and they can use different pairs.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, ace low, straights and flushes don't count against you. The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low *and* a straight, so it scoops.
- No qualifying low: high takes everything. Tied lows get [quartered](/glossary#quartered) (or sixthed).
- [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip): high half under WSOP's general rule; worst position under its button-game procedure. Ask.

## Watch out for

- Five suited in hand with only two suited board cards. No flush.
- A-2 is everywhere. Second and third [nut lows](/glossary#nut-low) mostly lose money, and [counterfeit](/glossary#counterfeit) risk is higher.
- Two pair and sets rarely win at showdown. The nuts, or close to it, does.
- Name collision: at Bicycle, and on Wikipedia, "Big O" means five-card Omaha *high only*. Confirm "8 or better."
