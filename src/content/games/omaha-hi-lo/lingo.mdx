## It's Omaha, plus a [qualifier](/glossary#eight-or-better) low half

Standard [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule). The pot splits between the best high and the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) that is [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better). No qualifying low means the high [scoops](/glossary#scoop).

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards, flop/turn/river, usually [limit](/glossary#limit).
- Two independent hands: exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, aces low, no straight/flush penalty. The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nuts.
- Ties for a half split that half, so you can get [quartered](/glossary#quartered).

## Watch out for

- [Counterfeited](/glossary#counterfeit) low cards when the board pairs your A-2.
- Boards with three or more cards above 8: no low exists, high takes the pot.
- Playing a one-way hand, like a pair of aces with no low draw, and getting stuck with half.
