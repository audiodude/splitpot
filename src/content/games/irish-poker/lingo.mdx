## Four cards, [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) or [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), discard two after the flop

Crazy Pineapple with four cards instead of three. **Rarity: low**, home-game territory, not in WSOP rules.

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards, bet pre-flop and flop with all four.
- After flop betting, before the turn: discard two, face down.
- Then plain Hold'em. Best five from two kept cards plus board, any combination. No [Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule).
- High only. Usually NL or PL.

## Watch out for

- Timing is post-flop-betting, pre-turn (Pagat, Wikipedia, Card Player). One PokerListings paragraph is looser, so confirm the table's version.
- Not Omaha: you can play the board.
- It shares a name with an unrelated drinking game.
