## [Ante](/glossary#ante)-up, no pre-flop action, flop out

A [bomb pot](/glossary#bomb-pot) overlays a base game's deal. It is not a separate ranking game.

## Cheat sheet

- Equal antes, hole cards, flop, action left of the button. No [burn](/glossary#burn-card) pre-flop.
- WSOP rule 31 ties it to time collection. Defaults: $20 (1-2-5), $25 ($5-$5 and up).
- WSOP: opt-out means not dealt in, and the button doesn't advance.
- Most common live form: [double board](/glossary#double-board) PLO.
- Ante usually 3 to 10 big blinds (PLOMastermind).

## Watch out for

- Opt-out rules vary: WSOP yes, The Lodge yes, PokerListings says players can still fold before the flop.
- Who chooses the game and what triggers it is room specific. The Lodge uses a rotating bomb-pot button at dealer changes.
- Ante is dead money, so random ranges multiway.
- Quartering in double-board versions.
