## Drawmaha with a high [dugi](/glossary#dugi) draw half

[Drawmaha](/games/drawmaha) skeleton: five hole cards, flop, one [draw](/glossary#draw), turn, river. Half to Omaha high ([2-and-3](/glossary#omaha-rule)), half to the best high dugi from your own five, board ignored. Usually [limit](/glossary#limit); WSOP allows pot-limit and no-limit too. WSOP cap is seven players.

## Cheat sheet

- Dugi half: four of your five final cards, four different suits, ranks may repeat, ranked as a normal high hand with aces high.
- Category order: quads, trips, two pair, pair, then four different ranks ([rainbow](/glossary#rainbow) no-pair, the weakest dugi). Any dugi beats any non-dugi.
- Nuts: A♣ A♠ A♦ A♥. Not A-K-Q-J.
- Typical winner (Haney): one high pair in four suits, e.g. K♣ K♠ T♦ 4♥.
- Omaha half: exactly two hole cards plus three board, high.
- WSOP draw: 1 to 3 or [pat](/glossary#snow), turn dealt before the draw cards. Ties split their half; a player can [scoop](/glossary#scoop).
- Odd chip: WSOP says first player clockwise from the button.

## Watch out for

- K♣ K♠ T♦ 4♦ has a pair but two diamonds, so it isn't a dugi.
- Trips dugi (7♦ 7♥ 7♣ 3♠) has little Omaha value and is hard to scoop with.
- The WSOP text only says "best hand A♠ A♥ A♦ A♣". The category order comes from Card Player writers, and how non-dugi hands rank against each other isn't spelled out in the sources.
- Not [badugi](/glossary#badugi) and not Badugi Hi-Lo. Low rainbow cards are the worst dugi here.
- Haney rates it 3/10 ("many detractors"): expect few rooms to spread it.

## Examples

Three players, final hands: Ada K♣ K♠ K♦ 10♦ 4♥ (trips dugi K♣ K♠ K♦ 4♥, the 10♦ shares a suit with the K♦), Ben A♥ A♦ 9♣ 9♠ 5♥ (two-pair dugi A♥ A♦ 9♣ 9♠), Cleo Q♣ Q♠ J♦ 7♥ 7♣ (pair dugi Q♣ Q♠ J♦ 7♥). Ada wins the dugi half, then Ben, then Cleo. The Omaha half goes to whoever the board favors.
