## [Stud 8](/games/seven-card-stud-hi-lo) with the [qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier) deleted and [Razz](/games/razz) order of action

Pot splits between the best high and the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low). Some low always wins, even a pair. Seven-card stud structure, [limit](/glossary#limit).

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Best five of seven for high; best five of seven for low.
- Low: A-5, aces low, straights and flushes ignored, pairs count against you. No [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) requirement.
- [Bring-in](/glossary#bring-in): **highest** [door card](/glossary#door-card), ace low, K♠ automatic. May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: **lowest** board acts first (WSOP rule 249, BARGE). Opposite of Stud 8.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth to seventh. No [open-pair](/glossary#open-pair) big bet on fourth.
- Odd chip in the total pot goes to high. Within a tied half, WSOP/TDA: highest card by suit among the five. Robert's: low card by suit for a tied low half.
- [Scoop](/glossary#scoop) possible. A tied low with someone else winning high is [quartered](/glossary#quartered).
- Splits are decided by the cards only, never by agreement.

## Watch out for

- A ten-low or a pair collects half the pot if everybody is worse.
- Haney calls big pairs "mostly unplayable" here. A low door card gets you action.
- High board acting first is the Stud 8 rule. Not here.
- Holding the king: forced in with the bring-in.
- Names: WSOP/PokerNews "Stud Hi-Lo Regular", BARGE "No Qualifier", Haney "Stud NQ", Wikipedia "Q". Resorts World's "(Three Cards Down)" version is unspelled-out and probably a discard-one game. [INFERENCE]
