## Stud 8 with a 4-down/1-up start, a forced two-card discard, and a high-card [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in)

Ante, 4 down + 1 up, bet, then discard two hidden cards before 4th street. After that it's stud hi-lo [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better): [limit](/glossary#limit), five betting rounds, small bet 3rd/4th, big 5th-7th. Half the pot to best high, half to best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) that qualifies.

## Cheat sheet

- **High** [door card](/glossary#door-card) brings it in (WSOP 250, Haney, CountingOuts). Haney counts the ace as low, so K♠ is the automatic bring-in. The WSOP doesn't specify the ace.
- Discard two hidden cards after 3rd street, before 4th. Miss it and you have a [dead hand](/glossary#dead-hand).
- No open-pair big bet on 4th street.
- Best high takes half, best qualifying low takes half. No qualifier means high [scoops](/glossary#scoop). Tied halves get [quartered](/glossary#quartered).
- Odd chip: high hand in the whole pot; highest card by suit within ties.

## Watch out for

- BARGE says low card brings it in; every other source I found says high.
- [Rolled-up](/glossary#rolled-up) trips and made lows are far more common, so play tighter than regular stud 8 (Badger).
- Your own discards are known dead cards.
- Cards running out: WSOP reshuffles the muck, CountingOuts says a community card is normal.
