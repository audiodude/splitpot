## Stud for the lowest [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low). [No qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier), whole pot, one winner

Seven-card stud ([ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), [limit](/glossary#limit)), played for the best ace-to-five low out of seven cards. The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nuts. Straights and flushes don't count against you. Pairs do.

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Best five of seven. Play five different ranks if you can.
- Bring-in: **highest** [door card](/glossary#door-card), ace low, suit tiebreak spades > hearts > diamonds > clubs. K♠ is automatic. May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: **lowest** board acts first. A paired board is worse than any unpaired one. Tie: closest to the dealer's left (WSOP, Robert's). TDA: high card by suit.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth to seventh. [Open pair](/glossary#open-pair) on fourth does nothing.
- Raise cap: WSOP bet plus four (completion not counted). Robert's and Pagat: bet plus three.
- Ties split. Odd chip: WSOP/TDA highest card by suit in the five. Robert's: lowest card by suit.
- Eight players: stub runs out, one face-up [community card](/glossary#community-card) for seventh street.
- "Kansas City Razz" in the WSOP text is [2-7 Razz](/games/2-7-razz), not this.
- Dealers announce pairs on board but not face-card pairs (Robert's).

## Watch out for

- The ace never plays high. A-A is the lowest pair, but it's still a pair.
- Count [dead cards](/glossary#dead-card). A 3-4-5 board with those ranks already showing in other hands is weak.
- A king bring-in is usually a fold. Completing a high bring-in with a low door card is the standard steal.
- Any unpaired hand beats any paired one, even a Q-high.
- Compare from the top card down: 7-6-4-3-A beats 7-6-5-2-A.
