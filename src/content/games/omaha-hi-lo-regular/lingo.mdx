## [Omaha Hi-Lo](/games/omaha-hi-lo) with no [qualifier](/glossary#qualifier)

[Two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), high half and low half, but the low is plain [ace-to-five](/glossary#a5-low) with no [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) requirement. The low half always gets paid. WSOP: "no qualifier for low."

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards, flop/turn/river, limit, pot-limit or no-limit as in O8.
- Exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each half; different pairs allowed.
- Low: A-5 ranking, no qualifier, aces low, straights and flushes ignored, compare from the top card down. The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nuts.
- Nearly every hand splits. A [scoop](/glossary#scoop) needs one player to own both halves.
- Ties are [quartered](/glossary#quartered). [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip) as in O8.

## Watch out for

- A board with no low still pays the low half, so don't fold the nut-low draw just because a 9 is on board.
- How a paired low ranks isn't spelled out in the WSOP text. Probably unpaired beats paired (our inference). Ask the floor.
- Only the WSOP live-action sheet defines it. Confirm there's no qualifier, and that nobody means a 9-or-better qualifier.
