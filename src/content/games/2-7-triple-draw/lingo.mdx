## Deuce-to-seven lowball, three [draws](/glossary#draw), [limit](/glossary#limit)

Five cards, button and blinds, small-small-big-big. Lowest hand by [2-7 rules](/glossary#27-low) takes the whole pot. No [qualifier](/glossary#eight-or-better), no split.

## Cheat sheet

- Aces high, straights and flushes count against you, pairs count against you. The nuts is 7-5-4-3-2 offsuit, the [number one](/glossary#27-low).
- Pre-draw action starts left of the big blind. Every post-draw round starts left of the button.
- Draw 0-5 cards. WSOP: discards go in first, the dealer announces counts, then the number is locked. Burn before every draw, even if everyone stands [pat](/glossary#snow).
- Big bet starts after the **second** draw. Multiway: bet plus four raises.
- Six max. If the stub runs out, reshuffle old discards and burns, never the current round's discards. The last stub card is used only if it completes the round.
- Odd chip (tournament): first seat left of the button.

## Watch out for

- A-2-3-4-5 is ace-high. 6-5-4-3-2 is a straight. Five suited cards is a flush. All three lose to king-high.
- Room differences: exposed cards on the deal (WSOP replaces all; Robert's keeps a 2, 3, 4, 5 or 7), five-card draws (WSOP five in a row; Robert's four then one), changing your draw count (live action allows it until a card is dealt; tournaments don't). Page follows WSOP.
- Say "pair" when you spread one. Miscalling a hand that makes an opponent muck can kill yours.
- [Snowing](/glossary#snow) works here. So does calling down a snow when you hold a smooth eight.
- Joining: wait, post the BB, or [kill](/glossary#kill-pot) for double the BB (WSOP). Robert's only lists wait or kill.
