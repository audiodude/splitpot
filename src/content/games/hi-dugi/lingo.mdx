## Four-card [badugi](/glossary#badugi)-style draw where the best [dugi](/glossary#dugi) is the *highest* hand and pairs are good

Four hole cards, blinds, best high [dugi](/glossary#dugi) (four different suits, ranks may repeat) takes the whole pot. No split. Per the Card Player Lifestyle Mixed Game Festival sheet, the only listing I found.

## Cheat sheet

- Dugi = four cards, four different suits ([rainbow](/glossary#rainbow)). Pairs allowed and good.
- Ranking: AAAA, other quads, trips dugi (AAAK is second best), two pair, one pair, no-pair rainbow, then non-dugis.
- Any dugi beats any non-dugi.
- Within a kind, higher pair then kickers, aces high ([INFERENCE]: not spelled out).
- Structure: four cards, blinds, three [draws](/glossary#draw) of 0 to 4, [limit](/glossary#limit) ([INFERENCE]: the sheet only says "standard betting rules of games with blinds").
- Poker Chip Forum example: A♣ A♦ A♥ Q♥ loses to 2♣ 5♦ 9♥ K♠, because the first has two hearts.

## Watch out for

- Not A-K-Q-J rainbow. AAAA is the nuts.
- Counting pairs before kickers.
- Treating it like Badugi: low cards get no bonus.
- It isn't [Badugi Hi-Lo](/glossary#high-badugi), which judges the high half by the lowest card.
- Unspecified: how two non-dugi hands compare, the [odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip), and the number of draws. Ask.
- "Hi-dugi" also names Drawmaha Hi-dugi (the real-room version) and, per an unread 2+2 snippet, a five-card badugi-plus-poker-high split game.
