## Three-board Omaha High, lowest river card eliminates a board

**Rarity: low.** Dealer's-choice territory. Same mechanic as [Derailment](/games/derailment), different packaging.

## Cheat sheet

- [Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), high only, three boards, three flops/turns/rivers.
- After rivers: lowest river card's board is out, then final betting.
- Pot splits half and half across the two surviving boards.
- Structure: [limit](/glossary#limit) at The Lodge, otherwise [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit)-style with [blinds](/glossary#blinds).

## Watch out for

- Tie for lowest river: Card Player Lifestyle eliminates all tied boards (the lone survivor scoops); WSOP Derailment says "by suit".
- All three rivers equal: one source says the pot goes to the dealer. Don't rely on it.
- Hole-card count not stated in the sources (assumed four).
- Nuts on the dead board is worth nothing.
