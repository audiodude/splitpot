## [Big O](/games/big-o) with a [door card](/glossary#courchevel-door-card)

Five hole cards, [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low. The first flop card is dealt face up before preflop betting; the other two come after. WSOP cap: 8 players.

## Cheat sheet

- Deal: five hole cards, one board card face up, preflop betting, then the other two flop cards.
- The exposed card is part of the flop, not a burn.
- Exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each half; different pairs allowed.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, ace low, [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low. No low: high [scoops](/glossary#scoop).
- [Pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit) is standard; PokerStars also spreads [limit](/glossary#limit) and [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit).
- [Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip): high half under the WSOP's general rule.

## Watch out for

- High door card plus a high flop means no low, which means a high-only game. A low door card keeps everyone's low alive.
- Burn procedure before the door card differs by source (WSOP: none; Mixed Game Masters: burn). Doesn't change who wins.
- Five-card holdings make nut lows common, so [quartering](/glossary#quartered) is routine.
