## Hold'em, with a room's worth of no-limit rules attached

Standard two-card, five-board, best-five-of-seven Hold'em. What matters at a mixed table is the [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) rulebook, not the cards.

## Cheat sheet

- Blinds, optionally antes. Heads-up: [button](/glossary#button) posts the small blind and acts first pre-flop, last after the flop.
- [Table stakes](/glossary#table-stakes). Bet or raise anything up to your stack, unlimited raises.
- Min bet = big blind. [Min-raise](/glossary#min-raise) = at least the previous bet or raise in size.
- A [short all-in](/glossary#short-all-in) doesn't reopen the betting unless all-ins add up to a full raise.
- Optional [straddle](/glossary#straddle), usually 2x BB, last action pre-flop.
- Any zero-to-two of your cards plays. [Playing the board](/glossary#playing-the-board) is legal but you must show your hole cards.
- Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) to first seat left of the button. Run-it-twice, only with unanimous consent, and only if the room allows it.
- Mixes: the no-limit slot in the 8- and 10-game lineups; the "H" in [H.O.R.S.E.](/glossary#horse) is limit.

## Watch out for

- Raise sizes that aren't legal and string raises. Declare the amount in one motion.
- Short-raise corrections: WSOP brings an under-raise up to the minimum. TDA has a half-raise threshold. The room decides.
- Not showing when the board plays.
- Assuming your home straddle, bomb pot, or run-it-twice rules carry over.
