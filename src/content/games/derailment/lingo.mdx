## Three-board PLO [bomb pot](/glossary#bomb-pot); lowest river card kills its board

**Rarity: very low.** WSOP text is one paragraph, so several details are inferred.

## Cheat sheet

- Double-board bomb-pot rules: [ante](/glossary#ante), no pre-flop action, 8-max, [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit).
- Three boards through the river. Lowest river card removes that board.
- Pot is chopped between best hands on the two surviving boards. [Omaha rule](/glossary#omaha-rule) per board.
- Hole cards unspecified. Four is the only count that fits 8 players on 3 boards. [INFERENCE]

## Watch out for

- When to remove the board is undefined.
- Not the UK "3D" game (2 hole + 1 per board).
