## Stud with 1 down + 4 up, four betting rounds

Ante, 1 hole card + 1 [door card](/glossary#door-card), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in) on the low door card (ace high, suit tie-break), up/up/up, best five-card high wins. Usually [limit](/glossary#limit), but [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit) and [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) are spread too.

## Cheat sheet

- Small bet first two rounds, big bet last two (BARGE). Best high hand showing acts first on later [streets](/glossary#street).
- No four-card straights or flushes. Pairs, two pair and trips are the only board hands.
- No-bring-in variant: high card opens and may check (Wikipedia).
- Not in the WSOP 2026 rules. Sources are Wikipedia, Upswing and BARGE. **Rare.**

## Watch out for

- Upswing lets a pair showing allow the big bet on round 2 and caps raises at three. BARGE doesn't.
- Bring-in is the low card, and the ace is high.
- Four streets, not seven. Most hands end with an uncalled bet.
