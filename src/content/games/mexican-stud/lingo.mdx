## Five-card stud, 41-card deck (no 8/9/10, one joker), [roll-your-own](/glossary#roll-your-own) exposure

Ante (or collection), 1 down + 1 up, four betting rounds, [limit](/glossary#limit). After each round, in action order, each player chooses whether to expose the hole card; next card goes down if you exposed, up if you didn't. Always exactly one card face down.

## Cheat sheet

- Highest up card opens, must bet the small or large limit; joker showing counts as an ace.
- Joker: wild face down, [bug](/glossary#bug) face up (ace or straight/flush completion).
- Rankings: five of a kind, straight flush, quads, **flush > full house**, straight, trips, two pair, pair. Straights skip 8-9-10, so 5-6-7-J-Q is a straight. Ace plays low for A-5.
- Button rotates; action starts clockwise of it.
- **Rare.** California card rooms, the WSOP rulebook, and little else I could find.

## Watch out for

- Full house loses to a flush.
- The [Wikipedia five-card stud article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-card_stud) describes choose-after-receiving, but WSOP and Bicycle choose before.
- Bring-in size varies by source.
