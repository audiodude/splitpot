## Hold'em with four hole cards, the [two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule), and pot-limit betting

Everything else is Hold'em. High only. Hi-lo Omaha is Omaha Hi-Lo.

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards. Exactly 2 hole + exactly 3 board. Six two-card combos per hand.
- No flush with one suited hole card. No playing the board. A set needs a pocket pair plus one on board.
- [Pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit). Max raise-to = call, then raise the new pot. Pot $100, bet $50: raise to $250.
- Exact math from $1/$2: open to $7. WSOP live rounding (SB counted as BB): $8 / four big blinds.
- Min bet = big blind. Say "pot" and let the dealer count.
- Table all four cards. Ties split, odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) to first tied hand clockwise from the button.
- [Nuts](/glossary#nuts) matter: second-nut flush and straight are money losers. [Wraps](/glossary#wrap) go up to 20 outs.

## Watch out for

- Hold'em reflexes: single-suit flushes, board-plays, trips with a hole pair and no board card.
- Top set and two pair are overplayed by Hold'em players.
- Pot rounding: WSOP rounds up the small blind and pot; TDA uses exact math.
- "Omaha" ambiguity: in Europe it normally means high; elsewhere it can mean hi-lo.
