## Seven-card stud with a 4-down, 1-up start and a forced two-card discard

Ante, 4 down + 1 up, bet [third street](/glossary#street), then discard two hidden cards before 4th street. After that it's normal 7-card stud: [limit](/glossary#limit), five betting rounds, small bet 3rd/4th, big bet 5th-7th, best five-card high of your seven cards wins.

## Cheat sheet

- Discard happens after 3rd-street betting and before the first 4th-street card. Miss it and the hand is a [dead hand](/glossary#dead-hand). Only hidden cards can go.
- Open pair on 4th street: any player may choose small or big bet (Super Stud High only, WSOP).
- Low [door card](/glossary#door-card) takes the [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in): the WSOP only implies this for the high game, BARGE states it. [INFERENCE.]
- Up to seven players. Deck is thin, so the muck gets reshuffled before 6th/7th (WSOP) rather than using a community card (CountingOuts says that's what many rooms do).
- Honest rarity note: the sources I found cover Super Stud 8 far more than this game.

## Watch out for

- Forgetting the discard.
- Hands being stronger: [rolled-up](/glossary#rolled-up) trips are about 1.3% of deals vs 0.24% in regular stud (CountingOuts).
- Your discards are dead cards you know about.
