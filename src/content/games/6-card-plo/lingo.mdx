## PLO with six hole cards

[Pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), [exactly two plus three](/glossary#omaha-rule), high only. Fifteen two-card combos.

## Cheat sheet

- Six hole cards. Exactly 2 hole + exactly 3 board.
- 7 players is the arithmetic max (42 hole + 8 burn/board = 50 cards). [INFERENCE]
- No WSOP rule entry for plain 6-card PLO. Everything else as in PLO.
- High only. Hi-lo six-card variants are separate.
- Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) by position clockwise from the button.
- Equities run close and the [nuts](/glossary#nuts) rule everything. Non-nut flushes are usually second best.

## Watch out for

- Three suited hole cards with two on board is not a flush.
- Four of a kind in hand is a pair.
- UK dealer's-choice games let the button pick 4/5/6 cards. Confirm the card count before you look at your hand.
- Don't call it "Big O".
