## Five-card triple draw, [hi-lo](/glossary#hi-lo) split, with a [qualifier](/glossary#qualifier) on each half

Button and [blinds](/glossary#blinds), five cards, three [draws](/glossary#draw), [limit](/glossary#limit). Half the pot to the best high (a pair of nines or better), half to the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) ([eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better)). Cards speak, same five cards both ways.

## Cheat sheet

- High half: five cards, standard rankings, must be **99 or better** (anything above a pair also qualifies).
- Low half: five cards, A-5 low, must be **8-high or better**. Straights and flushes ignored, pairs kill it.
- The [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low and a qualifying straight, so it competes for both halves. A made 6-5-4-3-2 does too.
- No qualifying high: best low takes it all. No qualifying low: best high takes it all.
- Nobody qualifies: WSOP rule 295 splits it among all remaining players.
- Usually six-max, because three draws burn the deck. When the stub runs low, shuffle old discards and mucks, but never the current draw's discards.
- Same pot can be a [scoop](/glossary#scoop). Tied halves get [quartered](/glossary#quartered).

## Watch out for

- **Qualifier variance.** WSOP, Pagat, CountingOuts: 99. BARGE and Badger: 66. Plus what happens when nobody qualifies (WSOP and Pagat: split among all; BARGE and Badger: best high takes it; some rooms carry it over). Ask.
- **[Odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip).** WSOP rule 153 says the high gets it. The WSOP's mixed-game notes say the worst position gets it. The Archie text doesn't say which. Ask.
- No free-rolling: unlike O8 or stud-8, a low draw has almost no high equity. A pair of nines or better shows up in about 27% of deals, an eight-low in about 2%.
- Don't overvalue 2-3-6 and 7-6-4-2 type draws multiway.
- Low straight draws are premium (qualify high, scoop potential).
- Breaking a made eight to chase a wheel is right early and often wrong later.
- It's not the same as Ari (adds a [board card](/glossary#community-card) for high only, 66 qualifier per WSOP) or the 49/0 versions.
