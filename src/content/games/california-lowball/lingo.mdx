## [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) single draw, with a [joker](/glossary#lowball-joker) and a [sevens rule](/glossary#sevens-rule)

Five cards, one [draw](/glossary#draw), open or fold pre-draw. Traditional structure is [limit](/glossary#limit), bet usually doubles after the draw. [No-limit](/glossary#no-limit) and [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit) exist.

## Cheat sheet

- Ace low, straights and flushes ignored, pairs bad. Nuts: the [wheel](/glossary#wheel) 5-4-3-2-A. No pair beats any pair; lower pair wins; kickers compare from the top.
- Must open or fold pre-draw. The BB may be callable, or the open must be 2x BB. New players can [kill](/glossary#kill-pot).
- Draw 0-5. WSOP: five in a row. Robert's: four, then the fifth after everyone else.
- Post-draw: first live player left of the button. Exactly five cards or the hand is fouled.
- Bug: the lowest card not in your hand. Never pairs you.
- Counts (BARGE): one wheel, five sixes, 15 sevens (21 seven-or-better), 35 eights (56 eight-or-better).

## Watch out for

- **[Sevens rule](/glossary#sevens-rule)** (limit only, Robert's assumes it): check a seven-or-better after the draw and you can only win the pre-draw pot; if beaten you lose calls too. BARGE's club version forfeits the whole pot. Not used in no-limit or pot-limit.
- [Check-raise](/glossary#check-raise): banned in limit A-5 by Robert's and BARGE; the WSOP permits it.
- Joker default: Robert's yes unless posted, WSOP no unless posted.
- Exposed cards before the draw (limit): Robert's keeps a seven or lower; the WSOP replaces all.
- Bet doubling after the draw: usual, but not in Northern California. Pagat describes a dealer small blind plus two big blinds.
- Say "pair" when you spread one. Miscalling a hand can kill it. Odd chip: first hand clockwise from the button, or worst position under the WSOP mixed-game simplification.
