## No-limit's rules with fixed bets

Same Hold'em, [limit](/glossary#limit) betting: [small bet](/glossary#small-bet) pre-flop and flop, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on turn and river.

## Cheat sheet

- Small bet = big blind. Big bet = double. $4/$8: $4 on the first two streets, $8 on the last two.
- A raise adds one bet size. No overbets, no min-raise math.
- [Raise cap](/glossary#raise-cap): WSOP is one bet plus four raises with 3+ players, unlimited if the round starts heads-up. It's a house rule, so ask.
- [Short all-in](/glossary#short-all-in) under half a bet does not reopen. Half a bet or more counts as a full raise.
- Buy-in is ten small bets. New players post the big blind. No button straddle.
- Some rooms run [kill pots](/glossary#kill-pot): full kill is 2x BB and doubles limits, half kill is 1.5x.
- Showdown as in Hold'em. Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip) to first seat left of the button.

## Watch out for

- Bet size doubles on the turn, not the flop.
- Trying to re-raise a capped round.
- Playing it like no-limit: you can't fold equity out, so play your draws and value-bet.
- Raise-cap disagreements: WSOP four raises, PokerListings three, TDA says the house limit.
