## Stud, plus an [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low half

Seven-card [stud](/glossary#street) structure ([ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), [limit](/glossary#limit)). The pot splits between the best high and the best [A-5 low](/glossary#a5-low) that qualifies. No low means high [scoops](/glossary#scoop).

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Best five of seven for high; separately, best five of seven for low (same or different cards).
- Low: five different ranks, all ≤ 8, ace low, no straight or flush penalty. [Wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nuts. Highest possible qualifier: 8-7-6-5-4.
- Bring-in: lowest [door card](/glossary#door-card) by rank, then suit (clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades). Ace is **high**, so 2♣ is lowest. May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: best *high* showing acts first. Tie: closest to the dealer's left (WSOP, Robert's). TDA: high card by suit.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth through seventh. **No** big bet for an [open pair](/glossary#open-pair) on fourth.
- If the best high board is all-in, action starts as if they'd checked.
- Odd chip in the total pot goes to high. Within a tied half, WSOP/TDA: highest card by suit in the five-card hand. Robert's gives a tied low half's chip to the low card by suit.
- Raise cap: WSOP bet plus four, Robert's/Pagat/PokerNews bet plus three.
- No qualifying low: high takes the whole pot. One qualifier: they take the low half.
- Ties for a half are split. A tied low with someone else winning high gets you [quartered](/glossary#quartered).
- Eight players: stub runs out and a [community card](/glossary#community-card) is dealt face up for seventh street.

## Watch out for

- Four wheel cards plus a 9 is not a low.
- Ace is high for the bring-in even though it's low in your hand.
- PokerNews' Stud 8 page breaks bring-in ties by position, not suit. Rule sheets (WSOP, Robert's, TDA, BARGE) use suit.
- Upswing says "below 8" for the qualifier but its own example treats 8-7-6-5-3 as qualifying. 8 or lower is correct.
- "Three Cards Down" at Resorts World is probably a three-hole-card, discard-one version [INFERENCE]; I found no full rule text.
