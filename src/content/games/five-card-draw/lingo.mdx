## One-[draw](/glossary#draw) high. Usually [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit) or [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit), sometimes [limit](/glossary#limit)

Five cards, [blinds](/glossary#blinds) (or ante in home games), one draw, standard high rankings, no qualifier.

## Cheat sheet

- Draw up to three normally. Standing [pat](/glossary#snow) is zero. Robert's allows four, with a five-card draw dealt as four, then the fifth after everyone else.
- Blind games: post-draw action starts left of the [button](/glossary#button). Ante games (Robert's): the opener acts first, or the next player clockwise.
- Limit: bet doubles after the draw. [Check-raise](/glossary#check-raise) is allowed in draw high (Robert's).
- [Jacks or better](/glossary#jacks-or-better): open only with a pair of jacks or higher, or any hand that beats tens. Split openers must be proven. If nobody opens, re-ante and redeal.
- [Guts to open](/glossary#guts-to-open): no requirement.
- [Bug](/glossary#bug): ace, or fills a straight or flush. In a flush it's the highest missing card of that suit. Five aces is the best hand.
- Cap: six or fewer players.

## Watch out for

- Exposed cards before the draw: Robert's draw high says keep them; the lowball sections and the WSOP say replace.
- Bug in or out: ask. Robert's says players are told; BARGE says the bug is usually in; WSOP lowball says no joker unless posted.
- Draw counts are tells: one card is two pair or a four-flush, two is trips, three is a pair.
- Four-flush or four-straight with one card to come is about a 4-to-1 underdog.
- California high-low split is described only by Wikipedia, with no room or WSOP source spreading it. Not covered here. Home variants (Jacks Back, deuces wild, trips to win) aren't covered either.
