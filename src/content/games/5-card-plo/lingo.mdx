## PLO with five hole cards, still [exactly two](/glossary#omaha-rule) plus three

Ten two-card combinations. Same [pot-limit](/glossary#pot-limit) betting as PLO. High only. Hi-lo five-card Omaha is "Big O" at the WSOP.

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards. Exactly 2 hole + exactly 3 board. Ten combos.
- WSOP caps these games at 8 players. [INFERENCE: nine players wouldn't leave enough cards for burns and board.]
- Winning hands are stronger than in PLO4. Equities run closer, and the [nuts](/glossary#nuts) matter more. Non-nut flushes and straights lose money.
- Usually pot-limit. At least one SoCal room spreads 6/12 limit.
- Run-it-twice with 8 dealt in: 2 burns, one per board.
- Odd [chip](/glossary#odd-chip): by position clockwise from the button.

## Watch out for

- "Big O" naming. WSOP and Commerce use it for hi-lo. Wikipedia and Pagat use it for either.
- Three of a kind in your hand is a pair. Four hearts in hand don't make a flush.
- Treating PLO4 ranges as if they carry over.
