## [2-7 lowball](/glossary#27-low) with one [draw](/glossary#draw), played [no-limit](/glossary#no-limit)

Five cards, [blinds](/glossary#blinds) (plus ante at the WSOP), one draw, one more betting round. Lowest hand takes the whole pot.

## Cheat sheet

- Aces high, straights and flushes and pairs all count against you. Nuts: 7-5-4-3-2 offsuit.
- Pre-draw: open or fold. Post-draw betting starts left of the [button](/glossary#button).
- Draw 0-5. Standing [pat](/glossary#snow) is zero.
- No [sevens rule](/glossary#sevens-rule) in no-limit or pot-limit. Any hand may check after the draw. [Check-raise](/glossary#check-raise) is allowed.
- Cap: 7 in WSOP tournaments, 6 in WSOP live-action.
- Odd chips and ties: identical hands split.

## Watch out for

- A-2-3-4-5 is ace-high. A one-card draw to 7-6-5-4 that hits the three is a straight.
- Room differences: exposed cards on the deal (WSOP replaces all; Robert's keeps a 2, 3, 4, 5 or 7), ante (WSOP tournaments yes; Robert's "sometimes"), limit forms (bet usually doubles after the draw). Page follows the WSOP.
- Two-card draws are rarely right. One draw means a missed one-card draw is a bluff-or-fold spot.
- Miscalling a hand that makes an opponent muck can kill yours.
