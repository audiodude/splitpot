## [Omaha Hi-Lo](/games/omaha-hi-lo) with three flops, two turns, one river

[Two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule) on a five-card board you build from one flop + one turn + the river. [Eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low, high [scoops](/glossary#scoop) when there's no low.

## Cheat sheet

- Four hole cards. Burn, three flops (9 cards), bet. Burn, two turns, bet. One river, bet. 12 board cards.
- Pick any flop + any turn + the river: six possible five-card boards (four under the [touching rule](/glossary#touching-rule)).
- Exactly 2 hole + 3 board for each of high and low; different boards and hole pairs allowed.
- Low: five unpaired ranks, all ≤ 8, ace low, [wheel](/glossary#wheel) is the nut low.
- Ties get [quartered](/glossary#quartered). The WSOP sheet doesn't list 3-2-1, so odd-chip handling is up to the room.

## Watch out for

- PokerStars' EPT Prague version was PLO 3-2-1, high-only. Confirm hi-lo.
- Touching rule: optional per Card Player Lifestyle, standard per BARGE (Four Card Chowaha). Confirm.
- Not to be confused with two-card Chowaha (Hold'em-style, BARGE) or Casino Chowaha (high-only).
- A river burn isn't stated in the CPL text; one burn per street is our inference.
