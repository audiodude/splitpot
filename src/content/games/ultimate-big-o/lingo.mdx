## [Big O](/games/big-o) on a [double board](/glossary#double-board), [best/best](/glossary#best-best)

Five hole cards, two full boards. Each hand is exactly 2 hole + 3 from **one** board ([two-and-three rule](/glossary#omaha-rule)). Best high on either board takes half, best [eight-or-better](/glossary#eight-or-better) low on either board takes the other half. Two-way split, not four. WSOP: 8 max.

## Cheat sheet

- Five hole cards, two boards (flop/turn/river each), ten board cards in all.
- High and low can come from different boards and different hole pairs.
- No qualifying low on either board: high [scoops](/glossary#scoop).
- Tied halves split; [odd chip](/glossary#odd-chip) to the high half under the WSOP's general rule.
- WSOP burns: one before the flop, none before a river that completes the board. Second-board procedure is inferred from the high-only Double Board Omaha text.
- "May also be played as a bomb pot" (WSOP), no further procedure given.

## Watch out for

- Four-way variant: Badger describes double-board hi-lo as commonly one hi-lo split per board. Confirm best/best.
- Cross-board [counterfeiting](/glossary#counterfeit): A-2 is only the nut low if no other player has an equal or better low on either board.
- Never mix top and bottom cards in one five-card hand.
- Don't confuse it with 5 Card Double Board Bomb Pot PLO (high-only, split by board).
