## [Razz](/games/razz) with [2-7 low](/glossary#27-low) rules

Same structure as Razz: seven-card stud, [ante](/glossary#ante), [bring-in](/glossary#bring-in), [limit](/glossary#limit), lowest hand takes the whole pot, [no qualifier](/glossary#no-qualifier). Aces high, straights and flushes count against you. WSOP calls it "Kansas City Razz", BARGE "Deuce-to-Seven Razz".

## Cheat sheet

- Two down, three up, one down. Best five of seven for the lowest 2-7 hand. Nuts: 7-5-4-3-2, not all one suit.
- Bring-in: **highest** [door card](/glossary#door-card), ace high. A♠ is automatic. May [complete](/glossary#complete).
- Fourth street on: **lowest** board acts first. Four up cards can't make a straight or a flush, so boards compare by pairs and high cards.
- [Small bet](/glossary#small-bet) on third and fourth, [big bet](/glossary#big-bet) on fifth to seventh. Caps, short-stub fallback and dead hands as in Razz and Stud.
- No open-pair rule is stated by WSOP or BARGE for razz games, so none is assumed. [INFERENCE]
- Ties split. Odd chip: WSOP/TDA highest card by suit among the five.

## Watch out for

- Ace is high. A-2-3-4-5 is ace-high junk, not a [wheel](/glossary#wheel).
- 3-4-5-6-7 is a straight. Haney's example: 7♥ 3♠ 4♥ 6♠ 10♦ J♥ 5♥ plays 3-4-5-6-10, a ten-low.
- A suited 7-5-4-3-2 is a flush. Drop a card and play it as a queen- or king-high.
- A pair beats a straight or a flush as a low.
- Stretch hands (2-3-7, 2-5-7, 2-4-8) are better starts than connected ones (4-5-6, 5-6-7) per Haney.
- "Kansas City" also names the draw lowball game. This is the stud one.
