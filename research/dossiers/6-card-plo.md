## Deal & structure
- PLO with **six** hole cards. Everything else (blinds, burns, flop/turn/river, pot-limit sizing, position) is the same as `pot-limit-omaha`. Fifteen two-card combinations per hand (RangeConverter).
- Seats: [INFERENCE, my arithmetic] 7 players x 6 = 42 hole cards + 8 burn/board cards = 50 cards, which fits a 52-card deck; 8 players would need 56. Pagat notes that a seven-player 6-card game "would not run out of cards" in the context of Binglao, consistent with 7 max. Live games reported at Aspers (London) are 7-handed (2+2 snippet).
- No WSOP live-action rules entry for plain 6-card PLO. WSOP does cap its 5-card games at 8 players and mentions nothing for six-card.

## Showdown / what wins
- High hand only. Use **exactly two** of the six hole cards and **exactly three** of the five board cards (Pagat, Wikipedia, CanadianPoker, RangeConverter all agree).
- Standard five-card poker hand rankings; ties split; odd chip by position clockwise from the button.
- Hi-lo six-card variants exist (e.g. in Pagat's "Binglao" die-roll game) but plain PLO6 is high only.

## Rooms disagree
- **Which card-count is the base game.** In UK dealer's-choice style PLO games the button picks 4, 5 or 6 hole cards for the orbit (2+2 forum posts about Aspers 888 Stratford and the Hippodrome; "almost all games are 6 cards" and "common to see players sit out if anyone dares choose 4 cards" are forum reports, not room rules). Elsewhere PLO6 is its own listed game online.
- **"Big O".** Pagat says five- and six-card Omaha games "are sometimes known as Big O"; WSOP and Commerce reserve "Big O" for 5-card Hi-Lo. Don't use "Big O" for PLO6.
- Max seats (7 vs rooms that cap at 6) vary per room.

## Common newcomer mistakes
- Applying PLO4 or PLO5 hand-selection charts directly (RangeConverter, PLO365: premium pairs lose value, equities run much closer, non-nut flushes are frequently second-best).
- Over-folding or over-calling because more flops connect with more hands.
- Forgetting the exactly-two rule when you hold six cards (the extra cards cannot be played together, e.g. three hearts in hand give no flush on their own).

## Lore & reputation
- Wikipedia: Six-card Omaha ("6-O") "can be found in many casinos across the UK"; some online rooms spread Five-card Omaha, Six-card Omaha and Courchevel. 2+2 posters describe Aspers 888 in Stratford running £1/£2 dealer's-choice 4/5/6-card PLO, 7-handed, with most hands played with six cards. These are lightly sourced; treat the room names as reports.
- Online: RangeConverter says six-card Omaha is "a relatively recent addition" that major and crypto-friendly sites added in waves; solid but vendor-flavoured sources (PLO365 is an affiliate/training site) describe it as the highest-variance Omaha game.
- The die-roll PLO game "Binglao" (Pagat) rolls a six-sided die to set 4/5/6 cards and Hi vs Hi-Lo before each deal; it has been played several times at the Norwegian Poker Championship week in Dublin. Variant note only; not important for a mixed-game rotation.
