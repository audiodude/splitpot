## Deal & structure
Same as `drawmaha`: 5 hole cards, blinds, small-bet pre-flop and flop betting rounds, 3-card flop, ONE draw after flop betting (BARGE: discard 0-3 cards), then turn and river at the big bet, then showdown. Limit or pot-limit. Draw cap varies by table (see below).

## Showdown / what wins
Pot splits in half, no qualifier.
1. **Omaha-high half:** exactly 2 hole + exactly 3 board cards.
2. **Zero (points) half:** add up the point values of ALL five hole cards; the LOWEST total wins.
   - Ace = 1; two through ten = face value; Jack, Queen, King = 0.
   - Best possible hand is any five face cards (0 points). BARGE adds that the worst hand is T-T-T-T-9 (49 points), which is the nut in `drawmaha-49`.
   - **Tens are worth 10, not zero** — both Haney articles call this "the common mistake".
   - Suits and poker hand ranks do not matter on this half. Equal totals split the half.
3. One player can scoop. Odd chip: by position clockwise from the button (the WSOP Drawmaha rule; WSOP does not name Zero itself) [INFERENCE for Zero].

## Rooms disagree
- **Name:** "Drawmaha Zero (0)" (Coach's Resorts World mix list, Cardplayer Lifestyle), "Dramaha Zero" (Haney), "Drawmaha 0" (forum posters).
- **Not in WSOP's rule text.** WSOP lists only High, 2-7, Ace-5, Badugi, Badugi High, and 49 for Drawmaha; Zero is a mixed-game-festival / mix-menu game.
- **Draw cap:** table-dependent; a Poker Chip Forum poster says "draw 3, 4 or 5 depending on the table"; the "Draw2maha" convention caps at 2. WSOP's Drawmaha text is 1-3. Turn-before-draw dealing as in `drawmaha`.
- Poker Chip Forum posters note a hazard: 49 and Zero look alike. One player describes having played 49 in his head while the table was playing Zero when the two sat close together in a rotation — don't put them back-to-back.

## Common newcomer mistakes
- Counting tens as zero (they're 10).
- Treating the ace as a premium on the points side only: ace is 1 point (very good) AND is the best Omaha card, so it's doubly valuable (Haney).
- Using Zero reasoning from 49: here you WANT kings, queens, jacks (12 cards) plus aces (4) — 16 "premium" cards — Haney contrasts this with only four tens in 49.
- Assuming scoops are rare: Haney and a forum poster both argue that the same cards (aces and faces) make both strong Omaha hands and low point totals, so scooping is easier than in 49.

## Lore & reputation
- Haney (Card Player, May 2025) rates Dramaha Zero 8/10 — his best of the three point games (49 = 5/10, 21 = 6/10) — because 16 premium cards create multiway action and big pots. He notes a typical good draw example: A♦K♠2♣3♦9♣ can become six points after swapping the nine for a face card and also has Omaha value.
- Poker Chip Forum posters are split (one says it "sucks", another enjoys both). Treat as opinion.
- Spread: on Coach's $8-$16 mix at Resorts World (Card Player, Jan 2023); on the Mixed Game Festival rule sheet; forum posters report playing it at a Dallas card room ("Degen") and in a poker-forum meetup rotation.
- Home-style cousins: Drawmaha 21 (`drawmaha-21`; BARGE lists it and ran it in the EMBARGO 2026 Drawmaha mix), Dramaha Red/Black (five of a single color) and Dugi 2.0 (3-way split), both home-game only per Haney. A forum poster also mentions "Drawmaha 6".
